Sculpted by Nature, Finished by Hand

Ember and Oak presents a radical synthesis of industrial materiality and organic warmth. Architectural furnishings designed for contemporary spaces.

Rooted in structural honesty and material depth.

Founded on the principle that furniture should serve as foundational architecture rather than temporary decor, Ember and Oak explores the tension between blackened steel and deeply grained organic wood.

Every silhouette is deliberately restrained. We reject excess ornamentation, allowing the native character, knots, and grain patterns of sustainable timber to dictate the personality of each creation.
